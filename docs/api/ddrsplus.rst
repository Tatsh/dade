Dance Dance Revolution S+
=========================

Readers for the ``.gen`` song containers of the Konami iOS rhythm game *Dance Dance Revolution
S+*: the ``KDEI`` framing around the shared ``BFCodec`` cipher, the container and its metadata,
the PowerVR banner textures, the ``#OFFSET`` gap measured from the audio, and the extraction that
ties them together. The step charts themselves are SSQ, read by :doc:`common`.
