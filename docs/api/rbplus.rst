REFLEC BEAT plus
================

Converters for the shipped assets of the Konami iOS rhythm game *REFLEC BEAT plus*: the two
Blowfish keys, the ``.rb`` tune packages whose every entry is enciphered, the RBFF note charts
inside them and the strip images they are drawn as, the downloadable texture archives and their
nested manifest, and the pipeline that converts a whole download in one pass.
