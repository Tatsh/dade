In The Groove (PlayStation 2)
=============================

Readers and converters for the PlayStation 2 releases of *In The Groove* and the *In The Groove 2*
beta: the ``PCKF`` packs, the ``.av`` streams of music and movies, and the simfiles, written as the
data tree of a StepMania installation.
